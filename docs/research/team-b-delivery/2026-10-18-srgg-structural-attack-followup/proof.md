# Exact claim and applicability boundary

Let `R=Z[X]/(X^n+1)`, `n=512`, `Q=15744063` odd, `ζ*=1−X^(n/2)`, and let the published Algorithm 7–8 verification matrix be `M=[2A | Qζ*e₁−2h]` over `R/(2Q)`. This form is independent of whether a particular seeded Setup root is accepted.

**Universal final-column kernel.** Put `δ=(0,…,0,Qζ*)`. In `R`, `(ζ*)²=1−2X^(n/2)+X^n=−2X^(n/2)`. Therefore `Mδ` has first component `Q²(ζ*)²−2Qh₁ζ*`, both terms divisible by `2Q`; every other component is `−2Qhᵢζ*`. Hence `Mδ=0 mod 2Q`. Exactly two coefficients of `δ` have magnitude `Q`, so `||δ||²=2Q²`. A public response norm bound admitting `δ` alone does not guarantee that adding `δ` to an arbitrary valid response stays under the bound.

**Restricted minimum.** For a final-column perturbation `Q·t`, zero commitment is equivalent (as `Q` is odd) to `ζ*·t=0 mod 2`. In `F₂[X]/(X^n+1)`, the coefficient halves of `t` are `(a,b)`, and multiplication by `1−X^(n/2)` gives equal halves `(a+b,a+b)`; thus `a=b` is necessary and sufficient. If `t` has any odd coefficient, one paired odd coefficient also exists, so `||t||²≥2`. If `t` is nonzero and all coefficients even, `||t||²≥4`. Taking `t=ζ*` gives `||t||²=2`, proving the exact restricted minimum `||Q·t||²=2Q²`. This does not show global shortest-vector optimality in `ker M`.

**Single-prime-power CRT floor.** Write `Q=m·c` with `gcd(m,c)=1`. A centered integer coefficient that vanishes in all prime-power components except `m` is divisible by `c`; if nonzero, its absolute value is at least `c=Q/m`. This bound is sharp at the coefficient-component level via coefficient `c`, because `c` is nonzero mod `m`. Requiring membership in a particular polynomial factor ideal may raise the minimum. A local solution also needs compatible projections and a global lift satisfying the relevant norm and verification/game conditions. No cost estimate follows from the floor or residue-factor degree.

**Actual signature probe.** A frozen test-only Table 4 PARAM-I first signature was verified before probing. Among 256 paired supports `X^iζ*`, choose sign minimizing the public norm. If the old last polynomial is `v`, candidate squared norm at pair `i` is `S+2Q²−2Q|v_i−v_(i+256)|`. The probe checked the exact matrix column, each row's zero product mod `2Q`, canonical encoding, unchanged recovered commitment, and the published verifier. The best candidate exceeded `floor(γ²)` and was rejected. This only describes that one run.

**Unresolved.** An actual Ring-HNF/CRT/subfield attack cost requires an explicit target relation/game, the distribution of the accepted seeded public root and projected secret/error, a local algorithm with success probability and operation count, a valid full-ring lift under the norm, and an accounting of query/abort cost. None is supplied by a factor degree, the coefficient CRT floor, or the public kernel alone.
